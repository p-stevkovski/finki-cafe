About us

Our story

FINKI Café & Bakery was opened by former students who wanted a place to study, eat well and not spend the whole budget. We started with one coffee machine and one tray of croissants.

Today we bake every morning and welcome the same student spirit every day.

Our philosophy

Fair prices

Quality that fits a student budget.

Fresh every day

Pastries are baked on site, never shipped in.

A community space

Study, work on a project or meet classmates.
