About us

Our story

As former students of FINKI, we opened FINKI Café & Bakery to offer students a place to study and eat well at a fair price. We started only with an espresso machine and a tray.

Today we bake every morning and welcome the same student spirit every day.

Our philosophy

Fair prices

Quality that fits a student budget.

Fresh every day

Pastries are baked on site.

A community space

Study, work on a project or meet classmates.
